# SONIQCX interactive sections

Creative authority: user explicitly delegated a full reimagination of the home
revenue and podcast sections, with full-width composition and micro interactions.
This is a two-section revision, not a new page grammar, navigation or hero.

Thesis: a precision instrument for turning conversations into outcomes. Preserve
Archivo and the SONIQCX blue, but give revenue a pale blue stage and the podcast
a contrasting ink broadcast deck. Supplied dashboards and real episode artwork
are the stars. No generated images or invented metrics.

Journey / feeling: recognize the revenue promise (confidence), explore the real
workspace (control), discover SHIFT HAPPENS (curiosity), select a conversation
(agency), watch or explore the archive (commitment).

Revenue: rejected the prior text/image split in favor of oversized full-width
REVENUE typography above a dimensional three-screen product stage. Five labeled
selectors change the active screen; pointer tilt and progress micro-animation
connect selection to the workspace. All five original screenshots remain.

Podcast: replace the rotating cylinder with an edge-to-edge broadcast deck.
Oversized SHIFT HAPPENS title, three dimensional episode artworks, active title,
watch action, and a segmented waveform tuner. Waveform buttons select real
episodes; arrows, swipe and keyboard offer equivalent controls. Automatic
advancement pauses on interaction, offscreen, in hidden tabs, and for reduced
motion. Never autoplay audio or an embedded YouTube player.

The two sections change composition, ground and interaction; surrounding page
structure and its existing fingerprint remain unchanged. The original
Scrollcraft engine stays untouched. Native React and compositor CSS cover this
interaction without adding GSAP or WebGL startup costs. Mobile uses one prominent
image with partial side previews and wrapped controls rather than shrunken type.

Verification: inspect desktop, mobile and reduced-motion pixels; exercise all
selectors, arrows, swipe, keyboard, pause, enlargement and links. Measure rather
than promise performance. Physical iPhone hardware is not available.
