Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. This records the revised direction after the user's request to prioritize Scrollcraft. Earlier implementation is superseded by this score.

## Evidence and authored decisions
1. Vibe. User: “the most cinematic 3-D amazing experience that has a solid flow and isn’t overcomplicated or confusing”. Direction: dimensional, assured, precise, human.
2. Journey. User: “I don’t want anything where you have to click play to experience the main header”. Authored order: establish the brand and revenue idea; demonstrate the layers working together; explain convert/retain/grow; show AI workflow; establish real leadership; offer a conversation.
3. Energy. Authored: quiet opening, one spatial expansion peak, crisp explanatory pace, quieter human portrait, resolved invitation.
4. Feeling. Authored below. The user requests “unforgettable and not cliché and typical”.
5. Signature. Authored: the exact SONIQCX Q opens into independent material slices, briefly showing the architecture behind a conversation, then aligns back into one mark. It works from native scroll with no play gate.
6. Range. Authored: graphic, cinematic, industrial precision. Official blue #61abbc, charcoal, cool silver. Archivo from current official site.
7. Structure. Authored: distinct scenes with one localized 3D hero. No global continuous flight, no hijacked scrolling, no repeated long pinning.
8. Assets. Official SONIQCX wordmark and Q submark; user-supplied Justin Jones studio poster. No synthetic people, video, invented statistics, or generated raster imagery.

## Grammar: Signal assembly
A new grammar chosen because the visitor is inspecting a brand promise, then practical evidence, rather than watching an entire film or operating software.
- Navigation: direct section jump links and one stable conversation action; optional mobile drawer.
- Sequence: one self-contained spatial identity encounter, a normal-flow outcomes ledger, a revealing AI stack, a photographic human spread, a held typographic invitation.
- Ending: a blue invitation plate, not a magnetic button or an empty fade-out.
- Bans: global fixed world, repeated pinned crossfade chapters, counters, scroll prompts, persistent fake telemetry, horizontal scroll traps, surprise navigation, decorative audio.
- Other grammars: filmic one-shot hides cuts and bans direct jump links; chaptered editorial forbids the requested dimensional media hero; live surface would pretend this is an app; continuous world requires geographical travel; typographic poster denies the requested 3D subject; catalog requires a collection; split stage requires a two-sided comparison; cutlist is too fast and forbids the single earned pin.
