# SONIQCX: Conversation into value
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The user asks for a complete, on-brand rebuild and explicitly permits a new direction without a 3D header.

## Eight decisions
1. Vibe: user requests “cleaner, sexier tech”, “cinematic and mind blowing”, “on brand”. Authored direction: sharp Archivo typography, deep graphite, official blue, cool white, precise signal linework.
2. Journey: user requires “every single section page and part of the existing website recreated”. Preserve the full original information architecture, exact leadership identities, full bios, application fields, legal copy, and original service destinations.
3. Energy: authored immediate graphic presence; quieter evidence; one signal-to-outcome transformation; useful explanatory content; personal human proof; decisive two-path close.
4. Feeling: curiosity → tension → clarity/awe → confidence → trust → intent. Causes are recorded below before any act scoring.
5. Signature: authored conversation-to-outcome spectrum. A field of overlapping conversation traces separates into three legible outcome lanes as the reader advances. The same visual language gives Voice AI a related, quieter presence.
6. Range: editorial technology, disciplined and graphic. No neon glow, gradient text, floating 3D Q, fake dashboards, generic stock people, or text baked into portraits.
7. World: distinct scenes and actual separate pages. The site is an information-rich brand publication. Native vertical scroll. No global camera flight, no media play gate, no horizontal navigation trap.
8. Assets: five unaltered official leadership portraits; existing exact wordmark and mark; current official content and Archivo fonts. No invented people or generated media.

## Journey and feeling curve
- Recognition / curiosity: a complete overscale revenue claim and an independently moving line field immediately communicate what SONIQCX does.
- Tension: a concise diagnostic spread exposes the mismatch between activity metrics and revenue.
- Clarity and awe, THE PEAK: the overlapping traces organize into Convert / Retain / Grow. The visual tells the operating model. One 2.1svh interaction, largest dedicated motion span, comes after quiet information.
- Confidence: ordinary reading sections explain four operating pillars and three revenue systems. All source details retained.
- Trust: real portraits introduce the complete team and link to their biographies.
- Intent: a split service invitation lets a visitor choose consulting or BPO operations, each with its real booking destination. The page ends on complete, stable content.

## Grammar: Signal publication
Multi-page brand publication. Persistent compact full-site navigation, clear current-page state, real page links. The home page combines a natural-flow graphic cover, diagnostic editorial spread, one interactive process plate, operating ledger, systems spread, human strip and service-desk close. Supporting pages are individually composed: process essay, voice demo, comparison, courtroom dossiers, company manifesto, portrait directory, application desk, contact selector and legal reading pages.
Bans: primary navigation pointing back to the old marketing pages, repeated giant pinned heroes, content hidden behind motion, invented live telemetry, fake submit success.
Other grammars: filmic one-shot/continuous world obscure the required page directory; chaptered editorial bans useful persistent full-site navigation; live surface is not the actual product; pure poster cannot carry all source information; gallery only fits the team; split stage only fits the comparison; cutlist is too abrupt for the business content.

## Layer contract
Backdrop: faint orthographic registration grid, small scroll translation.
Signal: sharp vector/canvas strokes at native pixel density, independent scroll deformation and slight pointer depth, paused offscreen.
Typography: semantic markup, stable reading position, above the far signal plane.
Foreground: solid outcome labels and a lower metric strip occluding the signal field; no text under a full-screen overlay.
Mobile: stacked headline and lower, shallow signal crop; reduced travel and one-column outcome lanes. No sideways reading.
