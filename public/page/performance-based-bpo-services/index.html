How SONIQCX Builds a Performance-Based BPO Program

Start With the Business Outcome

We begin with the reason the outsourced operation exists. What needs to improve, conversion, retention, resolution, customer value, revenue, or another measurable outcome? The program begins there rather than with a required number of seats.

Map the Customer Journey

We identify where the relevant conversations happen, what customers need at each stage, where friction exists, and where performance can be improved.

Define the Operating Model

The service scope, workflows, staffing approach, customer handling, escalation paths, technology, reporting, and quality expectations are designed around the engagement.

Define the Measurement

Both sides establish the performance measures that matter and how those results will be evaluated. The exact measurement model depends on the program.

Launch, Learn and Improve

Once the operation is active, interaction data and performance outcomes are used to improve coaching, workflows, scripts, routing, automation, and customer handling over time.

Frequently Asked Questions About Performance-Based BPO Services

What are performance-based BPO services?
Performance-based BPO services are outsourced customer operations built around agreed business outcomes rather than billing clients for agent hours. The service may involve sales, customer support, retention, lead qualification, appointment setting, account growth, contact center operations, or another measurable customer workflow.
What BPO services does SONIQCX provide?
SONIQCX provides performance-based sales outsourcing, inbound and outbound sales, lead qualification, appointment setting, customer service, customer support, retention, upselling and cross-selling, call center operations, contact center operations, and AI-enabled customer experience services.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance measures established for the engagement.
How is performance measured?
The measurement model depends on the service and business objective. Performance may involve conversion, revenue, qualified opportunities, retention, renewals, customer outcomes, account growth, or other agreed measures that fit the engagement.
Are all SONIQCX programs measured the same way?
No. Different customer journeys create different kinds of value. The appropriate performance measures and commercial terms are defined for each engagement rather than forcing every client into one universal formula.
Does SONIQCX provide both sales and customer support services?
Yes. SONIQCX provides performance-based services across sales, customer service, customer support, retention, and other parts of the customer journey.
Can SONIQCX combine human agents and AI?
Yes. SONIQCX combines human execution with AI-enabled tools and automation where they improve the customer operation. The technology supports the performance objective rather than replacing human involvement simply for the sake of automation.
Does SONIQCX provide BPO services nationwide?
Yes. SONIQCX works with businesses nationwide across the United States.
