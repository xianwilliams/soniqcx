What shapes the cost

What Drives Call Center Outsourcing Cost

Staffing Model

How many agents, seats, or how much capacity the operation requires.

Call Complexity

Simple transactional calls cost differently from complex troubleshooting or sales conversations.

Interaction Volume

Higher, steadier volume is priced differently from small or unpredictable volume.

Call Type

Inbound support, outbound sales, retention, and appointment setting all carry different requirements.

Skill Requirements

The knowledge and judgment required to handle the calls affect cost.

Customer Journey

A short self-contained call behaves differently from a multi-step process.

Reporting

The visibility and detail the business requires affect what the operation costs.

Technology

Systems, automation, and AI support influence the operating model and cost.

Outcome Measurement

Whether and how performance is measured and attributed affects the structure.

Attribution

How results are connected to the operation, when performance determines cost.

How Call Center Pricing Is Structured

Hourly

You pay for: Agent timeMeasured as: How many hours were usedEconomics: More hours can mean more provider revenue

FTE / Seat

You pay for: Reserved capacityMeasured as: How many agents or seatsEconomics: Pricing follows headcount

Per-Minute

You pay for: Talk timeMeasured as: How long the calls wereEconomics: Pricing follows call duration

Per-Call

You pay for: Completed callsMeasured as: How many calls were handledEconomics: Pricing follows call volume

Outcome-Based

You pay for: Agreed resultsMeasured as: What the operation producedEconomics: Compensation is tied to the result

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does call center outsourcing cost?
There is no single number because cost depends on staffing, call complexity, volume, call type, skill requirements, technology, reporting, and whether performance is measured. SONIQCX does not publish a universal hourly rate.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour for call center services?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes established for the engagement.
What drives call center outsourcing cost?
Staffing model, call complexity, interaction volume, call type, skill requirements, the customer journey, reporting needs, technology, and how outcomes are measured and attributed.
What are the common call center pricing models?
Common market structures include hourly, FTE or seat, per-minute, per-call, and outcome-based pricing. Each connects cost to a different unit.
Is outcome-based call center pricing more expensive?
Not necessarily. The cost is connected to results rather than time, which can make the economics more aligned with the value the operation produces. The exact structure depends on the engagement.
Why is there no universal SONIQCX call center price?
Different operations have different call types, complexity, volume, data, and outcome definitions. SONIQCX builds the commercial structure around the specific engagement rather than publishing a one-size-fits-all rate.
Can SONIQCX provide call center outsourcing nationwide?
Yes. SONIQCX works with businesses nationwide across the United States.
