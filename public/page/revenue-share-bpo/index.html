What Is a Revenue Share BPO?

A revenue share BPO connects provider compensation to an agreed share or percentage of measurable revenue that is attributable to the outsourced operation.

That revenue can be generated, protected, recovered, or expanded depending on the engagement. The share is calculated against attributable revenue using agreed attribution rules and verified through agreed data sources.

SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes, which can include an agreed share of attributable revenue.

Attribution and commercial governance

A Revenue Share Is Only as Good as Its Rules.

Shared upside depends on clear mechanics. A revenue-share agreement should define how the share is calculated and verified.

Eligible Revenue

The agreement defines which customers, transactions, campaigns, or revenue are included in the share.

Agreed Attribution

Attribution rules are set before launch so the parties agree on how a result is connected to the operation.

Verified Data Source

Revenue is verified through agreed systems such as CRM, billing, order, or subscription platforms.

Net Revenue and Reversals

The agreement defines how cancellations, refunds, and chargebacks are handled so the share reflects net value.

Incremental Baseline

When the engagement uses a baseline, compensation is measured against the additional value created above it.

Clear Exclusions

Revenue the operation cannot reasonably influence is excluded from the share where the agreement requires it.

The exact mechanics depend on the engagement. The objective is a share that both parties can trust and verify.

Frequently Asked Questions About Revenue Share BPO

What is a revenue share BPO?
A revenue share BPO connects provider compensation to an agreed share or percentage of measurable revenue that is attributable to the outsourced operation, such as revenue generated, retained, recovered, or expanded.
Does SONIQCX offer revenue-share compensation?
Yes. SONIQCX can receive an agreed percentage or share of attributable revenue as part of a revenue-share structure. The exact terms are defined for the specific engagement.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to agreed performance outcomes, which can include an agreed share of attributable revenue.
Is revenue sharing the same as pay-for-performance BPO?
Revenue sharing is one commercial structure within the broader performance-based model. The exact commercial structure depends on the engagement.
Does SONIQCX take a percentage of all company revenue?
No. A revenue-share agreement defines which customers, transactions, campaigns, or incremental revenue are eligible for the share. It is not a percentage of all company revenue.
How is revenue verified?
Revenue is verified through agreed systems such as CRM platforms, billing software, order systems, subscription platforms, or customer account records. The exact data source is defined for the engagement.
How is attribution determined?
The agreement establishes attribution rules before launch. These may include defined events, campaign attribution, customer identifiers, time windows, or other measurable criteria.
What happens when a customer refunds or cancels?
The agreement defines how cancellations, refunds, and chargebacks are handled, which may include net revenue calculations, retention periods, or clawback rules so the share reflects net value.
Can revenue sharing be used for retention?
Yes. Compensation can be tied to recurring revenue protected through cancellation prevention, renewals, payment recovery, and other retention outcomes when the engagement is structured that way.
Can revenue sharing be used for upselling?
Yes. The program can measure incremental revenue created through verified upgrades, cross-sells, add-ons, and account expansion.
How is revenue share different from outcome-based pricing?
Outcome-based pricing is the broader category that ties compensation to agreed outcomes. Revenue share is a specific structure that ties compensation to an agreed share of attributable revenue.
Is revenue share right for every company?
No. It works best when outcomes can be measured accurately, attribution is clear, SONIQCX has meaningful influence over the result, and the economics create sufficient value for both organizations.
Does SONIQCX provide revenue-share BPO nationwide?
Yes. SONIQCX works with businesses nationwide across the United States.
