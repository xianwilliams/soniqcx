What Usually Stays Internal

  • Product and company strategy
  • Customer data policy and access decisions

Decision signals

When Each Approach Makes More Sense

In-House May Fit Better When…

  • The operation is small and stable
  • Context cannot be documented
  • You want full control over every detail
  • Strategic ownership is the priority

A SaaS BPO May Fit Better When…

  • The operation is scaling faster than the team
  • The scope can be defined and documented
  • Outcomes can be measured and attributed
  • You want accountability tied to results, not hours

Clear answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a SaaS BPO better than an in-house team?
Neither is automatically better. An in-house team has deep, immediate context, while a BPO can provide defined, scalable operations with clear accountability. The right choice depends on what can be defined and measured.
What should stay in-house?
Product and company strategy, customer data policy, final escalation authority, and brand decisions usually stay with the client.
Does outsourcing SaaS operations lose customer context?
Not when the engagement defines data access and standards. Context is transferred deliberately rather than living in individuals' heads.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes of the engagement.
Can I outsource part of SaaS operations and keep the rest in-house?
The engagement scope is defined for the specific program. SONIQCX can operate within an agreed portion of the customer operations.
Is outsourcing SaaS operations always cheaper?
Not necessarily. The objective is not simply lower cost , it is connecting more of the cost to measurable outcomes and scaling the operation without losing accountability.
