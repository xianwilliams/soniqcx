Frequently Asked Questions

Should customer support use AI or humans?
The strongest operations usually use both. AI handles repeatable, defined work consistently and at scale, while humans handle ambiguity, empathy, judgment, and exceptions.
Will AI replace human support agents?
Not universally. AI changes which work agents do, but many conversations still require judgment, empathy, and the ability to handle exceptions.
What does AI do better than humans in support?
Scale, speed, and consistency. AI applies defined steps the same way every time and responds immediately, which suits repeatable work.
What do humans do better than AI in support?
Complexity, empathy, judgment, and exceptions. Humans understand nuance, adapt to context, and handle situations that fall outside a defined workflow.
How do I decide what AI should handle?
Start with the customer problem, the stakes, the volume, and customer preference, then assign repeatable work to AI and judgment work to humans with a clear escalation path.
Does SONIQCX use AI in customer support?

Does SONIQCX charge by the hour?
