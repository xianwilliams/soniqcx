Frequently Asked Questions About SaaS BPO

What is a SaaS BPO?
A SaaS BPO is an outsourced customer operations partner for software-as-a-service companies. It can cover customer service, support, technical support, sales, qualification, appointment setting, retention, and account growth, depending on the engagement.
What functions can a SaaS BPO handle?
Customer service, customer support, technical support, sales, lead qualification, appointment setting, retention, and upselling or cross-selling, depending on the engagement.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour for SaaS operations?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes of the engagement.
Is a SaaS BPO the same as SaaS customer support?
Not exactly. SaaS customer support is one function within a broader SaaS BPO relationship. A SaaS BPO can cover the full set of customer operations, not just support.
Why is SaaS well suited to performance-based operations?
SaaS value is recurring and usage, retention, and revenue are often visible in the product, which makes it possible to define and measure outcomes clearly.
Does SONIQCX use AI in SaaS operations?
Yes. SONIQCX uses human and AI approaches, combining AI-enabled tools with human judgment around the agreed outcome.
Does SONIQCX provide SaaS BPO nationwide?
Yes. SONIQCX works with businesses nationwide across the United States.
