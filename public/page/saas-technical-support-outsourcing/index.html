Frequently Asked Questions

What is SaaS technical support outsourcing?
SaaS technical support outsourcing is an outsourced support model focused on product and technical troubleshooting for software-as-a-service companies, built and measured around agreed technical outcomes rather than billing for agent hours.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour for SaaS technical support?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes of the engagement.
Does SONIQCX provide developer or API support?
SONIQCX provides technical service capabilities, but the exact technical scope is defined for the engagement. Developer and API support are not assumed for every program.
What is the difference between SaaS customer support and SaaS technical support?
SaaS customer support covers broader help with account, service, and product questions. SaaS technical support focuses on defined product and technical troubleshooting with clearer escalation boundaries.
Can an outsourced technical team escalate into our engineers?
The escalation path is defined for the engagement. The operation can escalate issues outside its scope into internal technical teams with the agreed context.
Does SONIQCX use a specific tier structure?
The engagement defines the issue categories, troubleshooting scope, and escalation path. There is no single universal tier structure applied to every program.
How is SaaS technical support performance measured?
The correct measure depends on the engagement. Relevant outcomes may include resolution quality, first-contact resolution where appropriate, escalation quality, repeat-contact behavior, or another agreed result.
Can AI support SaaS technical support?
SONIQCX uses human and AI approaches. AI-enabled tools can surface product information and identify patterns, while human judgment remains important for unclear or unusual technical problems.
Does SONIQCX provide SaaS technical support nationwide?
Yes. SONIQCX works with businesses nationwide across the United States.
