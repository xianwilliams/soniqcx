The boundary matters

What Usually Stays Internal

  • Sales strategy and target accounts
  • Final deal authority and pricing decisions
  • Brand voice and positioning
  • The sales process and qualification criteria the operation must follow

Decision signals

When Each Approach Makes More Sense

In-House SDRs May Fit Better When…

  • The sales process is still being shaped
  • Product context is hard to document
  • You want full control over every rep
  • The motion is small and stable

Outsourcing May Fit Better When…

  • The motion and qualification criteria can be defined
  • You want to scale without the hiring and management burden
  • Outcomes can be measured and attributed
  • You want accountability tied to results, not hours

Clear answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an outsourced SaaS sales team better than an in-house SDR team?
Neither is automatically better. An in-house team gives you direct control and deep product context, while an outsourced team provides a defined, scalable motion with clear accountability. The decision depends on what you can define and measure.
What should stay in-house?
Sales strategy, final deal authority, pricing decisions, and brand voice usually stay with the client. The engagement defines the boundary.
Does outsourced SaaS sales cost less than an in-house SDR team?
The decision is not primarily about cost. The more important question is whether the sales motion can be defined and measured, and whether you want accountability tied to outcomes rather than hours.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour for SaaS sales?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes of the engagement.
Can an outsourced team match our qualification criteria?
The qualification criteria are defined for the engagement, so the operation is measured against the same standard you would apply internally.
Can I keep some of the sales motion in-house and outsource the rest?
The engagement scope is defined for the specific program. SONIQCX can operate within an agreed portion of the sales motion.
Does outsourced SaaS sales hurt meeting quality?
Only when quality is not defined. When meeting and qualification standards are set up front, the operation is held accountable to that standard.
