SaaS Customer Support vs Technical Support

Two Different Jobs. Two Different Scopes.

SaaS customer support helps a customer with account, service, and product questions. SaaS technical support solves defined product and technical problems with a clear escalation boundary. Knowing the difference makes the operation easier to scope and measure.

When to separate them

Separate Functions When the Technical Scope Needs Its Own Boundary

They Can Be Combined When…

  • Technical issues are simple enough for general support
  • The escalation path is short and informal
  • Volume does not justify a separate operation

They Should Be Separate When…

  • Technical issues are frequent or complex
  • Diagnostics need a defined workflow
  • Escalation into internal technical teams must be structured
  • Technical resolution is measured differently from general support

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SaaS customer support and technical support?
SaaS customer support covers broader help with account, service, and product questions. SaaS technical support focuses on defined product and technical troubleshooting with clearer escalation boundaries.
Are customer support and technical support the same team?
They can be, but they often benefit from separate definitions. Customer support handles a wide range of needs, while technical support requires a defined troubleshooting scope and a clear escalation path.
When should SaaS technical support be separate?
When technical issues are frequent or complex enough to require a defined diagnostic workflow and escalation into internal technical teams.
Can customer support and technical support be combined?
Yes, when the technical scope is simple enough to sit within general support. The engagement defines where the boundary is drawn.
Does SONIQCX provide both?
Yes. SONIQCX provides customer support and technical service capabilities. The exact scope and separation are defined for the engagement.
Does SONIQCX charge by the hour?
No. SONIQCX does not charge clients by the hour. Compensation is tied to the agreed performance outcomes of the engagement.
